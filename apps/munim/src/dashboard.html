          Memo recipe
          fund cap=100 INR reply=<your utest1 address>

          The cap is the most the agent may ever spend from this deposit. reply is where encrypted receipts are sent.

          Or fund from the Noir Wallet extension
          Noir Wallet (testnet build) signs the shielded deposit; the memo is written for you.
